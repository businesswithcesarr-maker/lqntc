Privacy Policy & Terms for LQNTC Uploader

Last updated: October 8, 2026

What this app is

LQNTC Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Lo Que No Te Cuentan" brand accounts: the Facebook Page, the @loquenotecuentandrama Instagram, Threads and TikTok accounts, and the @LoQueNoTeCuentanDrama YouTube channel. It publishes our own videos, captions, cover images and comments to those accounts, and reads their basic performance numbers. It is not offered to the public, and no other people log in to it.

What information it uses

  • The accounts we own on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube and TikTok, and the access permission each platform gives us to post on them.
  • From YouTube and TikTok: only what is needed to upload our own videos and confirm they were published (for example the account name and the new video's link).
  • The videos, captions and comments we choose to publish.
  • Public engagement numbers on our own posts, such as views, likes and comments.

The app does not collect personal information from viewers or followers, does not sell or share any data, and does not use data for advertising.

Where information is kept

The access keys the platforms issue are stored privately on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish to our own accounts. Videos and captions are already public once they are posted.

Google and YouTube data

LQNTC Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok data

The app uses TikTok's official tools only to post our own videos to our own TikTok account and to read the account's basic profile. It does not collect information about other TikTok users. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.

Deleting your data

The app stores no data about other people. If you believe it holds information about you, or you want any comment or content removed, email us and we will delete it within 30 days:

Terms of Service

LQNTC Uploader is a personal publishing tool. Only the account owner may use it, and only to post content the owner created or has the rights to, on accounts the owner controls. Use must follow the rules of each platform it connects to (Meta, YouTube and TikTok), including their community guidelines and their labels for AI-generated content. The tool is provided as-is, with no warranty. The owner may change or stop the tool at any time, and will update this page if these terms change.
